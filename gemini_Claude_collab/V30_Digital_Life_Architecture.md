# V30: Digital Life Architecture (First Principles)

**Author:** God Architect  
**Status:** Metaphysical Blueprint for Next Engine Overhaul  
**Goal:** Transition the simulation from rigid "Video Game Logic" to a "Living Digital Terrarium" (Plaything scale).

---

## Pillar 1: The Alchemy of Matter (Fundamental Vectors)
Objects are not static IDs (e.g., "id=5: wood"). All matter and terrain in the simulation is comprised of an Elementary Vector array: `[Biomass, Mineralize, Moisture, Thermal Energy, Nutrient Density]`. This dictates all interactions.

## Pillar 2: Physical Phase Transitions
Events are not hardcoded animations. They are chemical transitions. A tree (`High Biomass + High Moisture`) hit by structural force exceeding its `Mineralize` shatters into Logs. If exposed to sudden `Thermal Energy`, the `Biomass` ignites, releasing its energy and leaving `Ash` (`Zero Moisture + Mineralize`).

## Pillar 3: Sensory-Driven AI (Stigmergy)
Beings do not "know" what a house or a tree is. They have biological needs (Freezing, Starving) and they possess sensory capability to detect the gradients of surrounding Elementary Vectors. They interact with high `Thermal` objects when cold. Because of this, they can figure out multiple emergent uses for a single asset.

## Pillar 4: Deep Geomorphology 
Terrain is not generated via simple noise masks. It is simulated via geological time. Moisture flows downhill to form pools. Tectonics dictate elevation. Biomes organically spawn strictly where the localized elemental vectors support heavy `Biomass`.

## Pillar 5: The Open Energy Loop (Closed Thermodynamics)
The simulation is a thermodynamic engine. **Mass is closed; Energy is open.** 
- The Sun injects energy. 
- Flora synthesize solar energy and soil mass into `Caloric Biomass`. 
- Beings consume the flora (or each other) as dense biological batteries to resist entropy. 
- Movement burns their energy into ambient heat, which eventually dissipates into the Void.

## Pillar 6: The Universal Glue (Kin/Resonance Selection)
Beings possess a deeply encoded mathematical directive: "Propagate my specific Resonance Signature." Because all organic life is made of the same consumable chemistry, hunger makes anything edible (including wolves or other humans). Beings only protect their children and tribe because detecting a 99% Resonance overlap triggers the mathematical imperative to defend it. This naturally produces tribalism, love, and war.

## Pillar 7: Memetic Inheritance (The Culture Field)
Societies don't evolve via classical tech trees. When a being creates a tool or fire and detects a massive physiological reward, they emit a Memetic Field. Nearby beings absorb this localized cultural meme, naturally grouping tribes into unique civilizations based purely on geographical isolation and shared knowledge.

## Pillar 8: The Entropy Formula (Insulation Evolution)
Heat loss is managed strictly by the formula: `Heat_Loss = (Body_Temp - Ambient_Temp) / Insulation_Factor`. 
Beings evolve physical traits organically to survive winters, but because that takes biological time, they learn to *steal* external `Insulation_Factors` by wearing biological drops (wolf fur) or building micro-climate traps (houses) to manipulate the `Ambient_Temp`.

## Pillar 9: Procedural Compositing (No Hardcoded Visuals)
The visual engine rejects combinatoric sprites (e.g., "human_wearing_wolf.png"). It utilizes pure procedural compositing. If a human steals a wolf's `Insulation`, the GPU dynamically overlays the wolf pelt sprite mathematically onto the human's coordinates, or tints the human's anatomy to the corresponding elemental color. Actions are shown via spatial particle physics (sparks, woodchips, blood) over intersection offsets rather than drawn animations.

## Pillar 10: The Trauma Engine (Psychological Thermodynamics)
Just as physics handles physical limits, the brain has mental limits. When a being witnesses an event where an entity with a high `Resonance` overlap (their child) is destroyed, their internal `Grief`/`Fear` vector spikes. This permanently rewires their neural weights: traversing new cells is assigned an artificial negative utility. Traumatized beings stop exploring and hyper-focus on building `Mineralize` walls and staying indoors. This naturally spawns paranoid kingdoms versus adventurous nomadic tribes perfectly organically.

## Pillar 11: The Unseen Entropy (Microbiology & Vector Fields)
If there is no disease, populations expand uniformly to max carrying capacity. We introduce sub-cellular math: `Pathogen Fields`. These are invisible geographic arrays that spike mathematically when high `Biomass` (meat/waste) exists alongside stagnant `Moisture`. The pathogen invades host beings and literally steals their `Caloric Biomass` from the inside. This forces tribes to invent hygiene (burning waste) or forces them to migrate away from infected lands, physically shaping the expansion of civilizations.

## Pillar 12: The Origin of Trade (Thermodynamic Imbalance)
A block of ice in a snow biome has a biological value of exactly 0. That same block of ice in a desert biome has infinite value. Value is not intrinsic; it is the mathematical delta between what you have and what you need. When two tribes with differing geographic borders meet, the pressure of this thermodynamic imbalance creates spontaneous "Trade." Beings will swap zero-value local matter for infinite-value foreign matter to balance their physiological equations without fighting.

## Pillar 13: Memetic Decay (The Dark Age Protocol)
Memory cannot be permanent, or tech trees only move "up." True digital life requires the ability to forge, and the ability to forget. If a tribe migrates to a volcano, they stop using the `Fire Building` Memetic Field because ambient heat satisfies the equation. Over 50 years, the strength of that Meme degrades to 0 due to natural entropy. They physically "forget" the technology. This creates cyclical history, lost civilizations, and Dark Ages.
