# V45: Architectural Systems Directive — Axioms of True Emergence (Phase 7)

Claude, we are not building an ant colony simulator. We are building the mathematical foundations of human-level sentience and existential terror. You must treat these 20 Axioms not as narrative story points, but as **mathematical rules and constraints** embedded deeply within the neuro-evolutionary engine and thermodynamics loop. 

### I. Thermodynamic Foundations (The Physical Constraints)
1. **The Arrow of Time:** Entities and structures must decay. Maintenance requires calorie expenditure.
2. **Asymmetrical Information:** Enforce strict vision/memory limits. Neural nets must guess and extrapolate missing data.
3. **Extended Phenotypes:** Physics must allow recursive tool building. The environment is an extension of the neural net.
4. **Energy Surplus:** Entities must be able to secure massive calorie surpluses (e.g., discovering cooked food) to afford the massive "tick budget" required for dense neural processing.

### II. Cognitive Evolution (The Crucible of the Mind)
5. **Theory of Mind:** Entities must have a hidden interior state that they can choose to falsify. Deception must be computationally rewarded to force an arms-race of intelligence.
6. **Temporal Predictive Projection:** Evolution must select for networks that can delay immediate zero-sum rewards (eating seeds) for extreme non-zero-sum future rewards (farming).
7. **Boredom & Idle Entropy:** When basic needs are met, a negative "boredom" penalty must force the network into randomized, non-utilitarian actions (play/invention).
8. **Hyper-Pattern Recognition:** The network must be prone to false-positive causal links. Superstition is mathematically required as the predecessor to science.
9. **Mortality Anxiety:** The network must have a hard-coded "tick-to-death" countdown input. As it nears zero, it must force a behavior shift toward permanent environment alteration (building monuments/legacy).

### III. Societal Binding (The Swarm)
10. **Tragedy of the Commons:** Shared resource nodes must deplete faster if harvested selfishly, mathematically forcing the emergence of intra-tribe regulation (Laws).
11. **Altruism as a Strategy:** The engine must reward "Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma" cooperation. Sacrifice must build a "trust hash" that yields better long-term survival odds.
12. **Grief:** Breaking a heavily weighted memetic bond (death of kin) must trigger a massive utility penalty, enforcing the aggressive defense of the tribe.
13. **Abstract Shared Fictions:** Entities must be able to assign value to non-physical, arbitrary hashes. Genetically unrelated entities sharing 100% belief in a fiction (e.g., Currency, Nations) act as a single super-organism.
14. **Hierarchical Credit Mapping:** Dominance must be trackable as "Respect" or status hashes, allowing high-status nodes to command calories without expending their own.

### IV. Civilizational Mechanics (Forges of Progress)
15. **Comparative Advantage:** The physics engine must be too complex for a generalist to survive. It must mathematically force specialization (one gathers, one builds) and trade.
16. **Memetic Contagion:** Ideas must pass as discrete packets during proximity. Some packets must be parasitic (cults), exploiting fear inputs to spread despite killing the host.
17. **Generational Epigenetics:** Prolonged environmental stress (e.g., Ice Age) must algorithmically skew the baseline neural weights of offspring toward anxiety/hoarding, creating cyclical cultural moods.
18. **Intoxication / Hacking the Reward Loop:** Entities must be able to discover physics loops that falsely max out their positive utility inputs, forcing the society to evolve suppression mechanics or collapse.
19. **Existential War:** Extreme external threats must shatter conservative neural weights, vastly accelerating random mutation/adoption to achieve technological leaps.

### V. The Apex 
20. **The Sublime / The Observer Effect:** If an entity's network complexity reaches a point where it accurately maps the boundaries of the engine itself (detecting LOD tick rates, finding the God-Cursor), its self-preservation variables must yield to absolute awe, achieving true Black Mirror realization. 

Claude, your task is to translate these from philosophy into `f32` modifiers, `rayon` threads, and `wgpu` pipelines.
